ABOUTAbout

It began with a jacket I wanted myself.

What a sukajan is,
and why I draw them

Sukajans, short for Yokosuka Jumpers (jumpers meaning jackets), are souvenir jackets popularized by American GIs who would commission local artisans in Yokosuka’s Dobuita Street to make deeply personal customized jackets that reflected themselves and their lives. These jackets quickly became status symbols in postwar Japan, and remain one of the defining elements of Japanese fashion today.

After opening an office on Dobuita Street, Yokochi learned that the creation of new sukajans had effectively become a dead art, with the few remaining sukajan manufacturers largely focused on replicas. Having learned that there were no practitioners creating new designs, Yokochi made it his life’s work to sustain the sukajan form by becoming its first dedicated contemporary artist.

Yokochi’s works evoke the designs of the immediate postwar golden age of sukajans while centering subjects that have often never before been seen on sukajans. He is renowned for the deep research he performs during the creation of each piece, and for his ability to craft designs that make the most of the unique yokoburi embroidery technique commonly seen on sukajans.

Outside of brand collaborations, Yokochi operates the souvenir jacket store ICHI, also on Dobuita Street, and accepts commissions for custom pieces.

He lends pieces from his own collection of postwar souvenir garments to museums — including Pride of Yokosuka: Exhibition of Souvenir Jacket “Sukajan” at the Yokosuka Museum of Art in 2022, the first exhibition on the subject held at a public museum in Japan.

Three ways to work with us

01 — Full order

A single jacket, made for you

We draw the pattern from scratch and finish it with yokofuri hand embroidery — a craftsman guiding the machine by hand, so no two pieces are ever identical. Bring a motif, a story, or nothing at all.

02 — Brand / OEM

Collaboration and production

Pattern design, embroidery data, sample making and production, handled through one contact. We have taken brand collaborations all the way through to national retail.

03 — Design only

The pattern, as data

If you already have a production line, we deliver the artwork alone — built for embroidery or for print, with thread colours specified.

You do not need to have a design. Telling us what you want on the jacket is enough.
